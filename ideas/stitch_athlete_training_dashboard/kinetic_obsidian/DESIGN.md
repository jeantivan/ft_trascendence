## Brand & Style

This design system embodies high-performance distance running: precision, sustained discipline, and electric athletic energy. It merges the technical clarity of advanced sports chronometry with modern editorial design, avoiding generic corporate SaaS aesthetics in favor of a focused, premium athletic identity.

### Emotional Target & Audience
- **Target Audience:** Dedicated endurance runners, club managers, and elite amateur athletes striving for progression, structured periodization, and community camaraderie.
- **Emotional Response:** Elevated focus, athletic credibility, kinetic propulsion, and unwavering clarity under physical fatigue. Interfaces should evoke high-end carbon footwear, asphalt at dusk, and neon course markers.

### Visual Aesthetic
- **Style:** High-Contrast Performance Minimalist infused with Obsidian Deep Layers. Crisp mathematical spacing, dense technical data tables, and high-visibility kinetic accents against deep slate foundations.
- **Atmosphere:** Deep obsidian frames the canvas, punctuated by an unmistakable high-voltage volt green (`#CCFF00`) and surgical cyan pacing accents that command immediate action and instant glanceability.

## Colors

The palette is engineered around an obsidian bedrock with targeted high-chroma beacons designed for effortless optical recognition during indoor planning or bright outdoor tracking.

### Functional Assignment
- **Primary (`#CCFF00` - Kinetic Volt):** Reserved strictly for high-priority interactive states, primary execution CTAs (e.g., "Iniciar Sesión", "Confirmar Asistencia"), and performance highlights.
- **Secondary (`#00F0FF` - Aero Cyan):** Pacing metrics, active group progress, and structural status links.
- **Tertiary (`#10B981` - Recovery Emerald):** Completion confirmations, verified training thresholds, and base aerobic states.
- **Neutral & Surfaces:** Base canvas starts at `#090D12`, elevating onto `#131923` for performance cards, separated by crisp hairline boundaries (`#253047`).

### Domain Specific Badges
- **Session Types:**
  - `rodaje` (Base Run): Aero Cyan (`#00F0FF`) with 12% alpha fill.
  - `intervalos` (Intervals): Crimson Volt (`#FF3366`) indicating cardiovascular threshold.
  - `cuestas` (Hill Repeats): Gradient Amber (`#FF8800`) highlighting power and elevation.
  - `tirada larga` (Long Run): Deep Violet (`#A855F7`) signifying endurance capacity.
  - `fartlek` (Speed Play): Kinetic Volt (`#CCFF00`) with high-contrast obsidian typography.
- **RPE (Rate of Perceived Exertion 1–10):**
  - `1–3` (Recovery / Suave): `#10B981`
  - `4–6` (Aerobic / Moderado): `#EAB308`
  - `7–8` (Threshold / Duro): `#F97316`
  - `9–10` (Max Effort / Extenuante): `#EF4444`
- **Volume Alert:** `#FFB800` warning badge displaying spike indicators (>10% weekly distance creep).

## Typography

The typographic balance combines the technical, geometric posture of **Space Grotesk** for numerical data, titles, metrics, and badges, with the neutral legibility of **Hanken Grotesk** for long-form manager notes, session outlines, and group chats.

### Hierarchy Principles
- **Data Display:** Numerical values (splits, paces, weekly distance, km/h, and intervals) always render using Space Grotesk with tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to prevent optical wobble during layout updates.
- **Labels & Tags:** Category badges (e.g., `RODAJE`, `RPE 8`, `MARATÓN SUB-3h`) use `label-caps` with uppercase transformation and tracking (`letterSpacing: 0.08em`) to enforce technical precision.
- **Reading Comfort:** Athlete reflections and private manager feedback cards use `body-md` in Hanken Grotesk with balanced contrast (`#F8FAFC` on `#131923`) ensuring comfortable reading in dim trackside lighting conditions.

## Layout & Spacing

A modular 12-column grid system anchors the dashboard experience on desktop, scaling cleanly down to 8 columns on tablet and 4 columns on mobile devices.

### Layout Rules
- **Desktop Navigation & Shell:** Persistent left rail (width: `260px` or collapsed icon rail at `72px`) for switching between Club Hub, Training Calendar, Group Rosters, and Real-Life Events. Main content caps at `1440px` with fluid column scaling.
- **Spacing Cadence:** Based on an uncompromising 4px/8px incremental scale. Element gaps inside metric widgets adhere strictly to `space-sm` (8px) and `space-md` (16px).
- **Responsive Adaptation:**
  - *Mobile (<768px):* Calendar transforms from monthly grid to horizontal daily snap carousel. Margins compress to `1rem`. Badges and action buttons expand to thumb-friendly touch targets (min height 48px).
  - *Tablet (768px - 1024px):* 2-column split (Left: Weekly plan & metrics; Right: Next Event & Group roster).
  - *Desktop (>1024px):* 3-tier view with persistent metric summary, high-density weekly timetable, and expandable chat/feedback side drawer.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not rely on muddy, generic drop shadows. Instead, it uses stacked structural surfaces and luminous perimeter tracing.

### Depth Mechanics
- **Ground Floor (`#090D12`):** Primary canvas background. Zero elevation.
- **Surface Tier 1 (`#131923`):** Performance cards, session rows, calendar blocks. Outlined by hairline border `#253047` (1px solid).
- **Surface Tier 2 (`#1E2638`):** Hover states, active dropdown trays, spot request modals, and flyout sheets.
- **Kinetic Glow (Active States):** High-priority components (e.g., active running session in progress, next upcoming Event) receive a directional diffuse ambient glow: `0px 0px 24px -4px rgba(204, 255, 0, 0.18)`.
- **Dividers:** Thin, low-contrast rules (`1px solid #253047`) cleanly demarcate sub-sections without breaking visual continuity.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a confident, refined athletic profile using level 2 roundedness (`0.5rem` / 8px for standard components, `1rem` / 16px for primary performance cards, and full pills for badges).

### Shape Application
- **Performance Card Widgets:** `rounded-lg` (16px) with an inner padding of `space-lg` (24px) creates an engineered casing reminiscent of precision sports watches and telemetry gear.
- **Interactive Controls (Inputs, Buttons):** `rounded-md` (8px) provides solid structural grounding and crisp cornering.
- **Status Pills & Chips:** Fully pill-shaped (`9999px`) to immediately distinguish data descriptors, workout tags, and numerical ratings from interactive rectangular buttons.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Kinetic Action:** Volt background (`#CCFF00`), jet obsidian text (`#090D12`), Space Grotesk Bold, 48px height. On hover: subtle scale `1.01` and glow `0px 0px 18px rgba(204, 255, 0, 0.35)`.
- **Secondary Ghost Action:** Transparent background with hairline border `#374563`, text `#F8FAFC`. On hover: border `#CCFF00`, text `#CCFF00`, surface tint `rgba(204, 255, 0, 0.05)`.
- **Destructive/Emergency:** `#1E2638` surface, border `#EF4444`, text `#EF4444`.

### Data Tags & Badges
- **Session Type Badges:** Pill-shaped, uppercase `label-caps`. Rendered with a 15% opacity background of the session accent color and an opaque matching border.
  - *Example (Intervalos):* Background `rgba(255, 51, 102, 0.12)`, border `#FF3366`, text `#FF3366`.
- **RPE Rating Badges (1–10):** Fixed-width circular or rounded-pill tags featuring a high-visibility numeric indicator. RPE 1–3 green, 4–6 yellow/amber, 7–8 orange, 9–10 red.
- **Volume Alert Component:** Inline notification badge combining a warning icon, high-chroma Amber `#FFB800` accent, and crisp percentage label (+12% vs. semana anterior).

### Performance Cards & Session Blocks
- **Session Card:** Elevated container (`#131923`) featuring a left vertical kinetic status spine (4px solid color denoting Session Type). Includes athlete status indicator (Completed icon vs. Pending), scheduled date/time, and target distance.
- **Metric Widgets:** Surface `#131923`, large numerical typography `metric-huge`, small tracking subtitle `label-caps` in `#94A3B8`.

### Inputs & Selectors
- **Form Controls:** Dark slate fill (`#1E2638`), 1px border (`#253047`), text `#F8FAFC`. Focus state transitions border to `#CCFF00` with zero blur ring for surgical precision.
- **RPE Rating Slider / Selector:** 10-step segmented button bar or continuous slider with track-fill changing dynamically across the recovery-to-max effort gradient.

### Event & Spot Request Module
- Card displaying Event title, meetup location, confirmed attendees counter, and Manager badge.
- Status states for Athletes: "Solicitar Plaza", "Plaza Solicitada" (amber pulse), "Plaza Confirmada" (volt check with unlocked Event Chat button).
